Senior Psychological Consultant

Dr. Julian Thorne, PhD

With over 15 years of clinical practice, I specialize in providing an empathetic, science-backed approach to mental wellness. My practice is built on the foundation of psychological safety, helping individuals navigate life's most complex emotional landscapes.

About Me

My professional philosophy is rooted in the belief that effective therapy is a collaborative partnership. I utilize an integrative approach that combines Evidence-Based Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) with Trauma-Informed Care to address the root causes of psychological distress.

Throughout my career, I have dedicated myself to creating spaces where patients feel deeply heard and supported. I believe in the power of psychological resilience and work tirelessly to provide my clients with the tools they need to achieve sustainable emotional health and long-term well-being.
